Future doesn't run on points or coins — the shop is priced directly in **tracked
hours**. Fifty honest hours in Hackatime is fifty hours of buying power.

## How it works

1. Hours accumulate as you work, tracked by Hackatime on your linked projects.
2. The shop on your program home lists each item with its price in hours.
3. Order it there. Stock, shipping estimates and prices are all shown per item and
   change over time, which is why none of them are repeated here.

The stock leans into the theme — the kind of hardware that belongs in a program
about futures, from small carryable things to genuinely expensive gadgets. Go look;
it's more fun than a list on a docs page.

> [!NOTE]
> Only tracked, non-AI hours count toward what you can spend. See
> [Devlogs and hours](/future/devlogs-and-hours).

## Before you spend

- **Some items ship domestically only.** Where that's true, ordering from outside
  that country is usually handled as a grant in USD for the equivalent instead —
  the item's page says so.
- **Prices move** as stock changes. The number on the page at the moment you order
  is the real one.
- **Hours you spend are spent.** Plan around the thing you actually want rather
  than the first thing you can afford.

## If something's wrong with an order

Your Profile page has the links for getting help, and that's the fastest way to
reach the people running the program. **#future-ysws** works too, especially if
you're not sure whether something's a bug or just how it works.
