## Can I use AI to build my project?

A little — up to 30% of the project. Beyond that you can't submit it. Two things
to know: you only get credit for your non-AI hours, and there must be no
AI-generated text or images in the project. Even under 30%, something that looks
vibecoded can still be rejected. Build something you're actually proud of.

## What actually counts as "futuristic"?

Your future, the one we were promised, or one that never arrived. It has to read as
coming *from* a future — a look, a material, an interface, a mood you committed to.
[Pick a project](/future/pick-a-project) has three worlds to build from and real
examples of each. If you're unsure, ask in **#future-ysws** before you spend a week
on it.

## Do I really have to include the word "future"?

Yes. In the name, the interface, or the README — somewhere a reviewer sees it. It's
a pass-or-fail rule and it's the easiest one to satisfy.

## How long does review take?

Usually a few days, depending on how busy the program is. You'll see the status
change on your project or devlog once it's been looked at, and you can post devlogs
in the meantime.

## What counts as a shipped project?

An open-source GitHub repo plus a live link a reviewer can open. Hardware projects
can use schematics, a build video or similar in place of the live link — see
[Hardware and electronics](/future/hardware).

## How do I submit?

Open the Projects page, create your project with its name, description, code URL
and live/demo link, link the Hackatime projects you tracked time in, and hit
**Submit for review**. The full checklist is in
[Submitting your project](/future/submitting).

## Can I build hardware instead of software?

Yes — electronics is one of the program's project types, alongside websites and
software. CAD-only projects count too.

## Can I lose access or get banned?

Yes, this program is moderated. Abusing the mini-games, submitting plagiarised
projects, or otherwise breaking the rules can get your account restricted by an
admin.

## My hours look wrong

Say so in **#future-ysws** while it's recent. It's fixable then and effectively not
fixable at submission time.

## Who do I contact if something looks wrong?

Your Profile page has the links for getting help — the fastest way to reach the
people running this program.
